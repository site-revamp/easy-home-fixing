# Easy Home Fixing

## Website Overview

Easy Home Fixing is a Dubai-based home maintenance and technical services provider offering plumbing, electrical, air conditioning, painting, tiling and general property repair services.

The business serves homeowners, tenants, landlords and property managers who require residential maintenance and repair solutions. The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting its multi-service offering clearly, improving accessibility to essential business information and creating a more direct enquiry experience.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Improved Website Accessibility and Reliability

- Addressed the website accessibility concern identified during the original inspection.
- Prioritised a straightforward homepage structure that makes essential business information accessible.
- The original website returned a 403 access response during inspection, highlighting the need to investigate website access and reliability.

### 2. Clearer Service Presentation

- Organised the main services into distinct categories covering plumbing, electrical, air conditioning, painting, tiling and general home maintenance.
- Improved the presentation of individual repair and maintenance solutions.
- Addressed the previous lack of clear service communication, helping visitors identify relevant services more quickly.

### 3. Stronger Conversion and Contact Journey

- Improved the visibility of essential contact information, including the business telephone number and email address.
- Made enquiry opportunities and contact pathways easier to identify.
- Focused on reducing unnecessary steps between discovering a service and contacting the business.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Created a cleaner, more professional appearance appropriate for a residential maintenance business.
- **Layout Structure:** Improved homepage section organisation and service hierarchy.
- **Service Navigation:** Made individual maintenance categories easier to browse and understand.
- **User Experience:** Prioritised accessible business information and straightforward enquiry pathways.
- **Content Consistency:** Established a more consistent presentation of services and business details.
- **Conversion Focus:** Gave greater prominence to contact information and opportunities to enquire.

## Overall Direction

The redesigned homepage positions Easy Home Fixing as a convenient, multi-service home maintenance provider in Dubai. The improvements focus on clearer service communication, better website accessibility and a simpler contact journey, helping residential customers understand available services and take the next step towards arranging maintenance or repairs.
